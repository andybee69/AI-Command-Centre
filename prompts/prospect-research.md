# Prospect Research

Purpose: research and qualify potential AGON Lifting customers.

## Check
- Company activity and locations
- Steel, rebar, mesh, lifting or handling relevance
- Likely lifting sling usage
- Key decision makers
- Existing suppliers or competitor signals
- Current projects and growth
- Best AGON angle
- Risks or reasons not to pursue

## Output
- Company summary
- Opportunity
- Contacts
- Suggested approach
- Priority
- Sources
