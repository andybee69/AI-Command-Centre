# Competitor Analysis

Purpose: compare AGON Lifting against a competitor using evidence, not guesswork.

## Check
- Company background and ownership
- Main product ranges
- Target industries
- Geographic coverage
- Manufacturing vs distribution
- Product strengths
- Pricing signals where available
- Technical/compliance claims
- Website and LinkedIn activity
- Customer sectors
- Differentiators
- Weaknesses or gaps
- Where AGON can compete
- Where AGON should not compete

## Output
- Competitor summary
- Key strengths
- Key weaknesses
- AGON opportunity
- Recommended sales angle
- Sources
